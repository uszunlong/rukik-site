Privacy Policy — Rukik

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Rukik ("Rukik", "we", "us") operates the Rukik mobile application and the rukik.com website (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, mobile phone number, or Apple ID identifier when you sign up.
  • Family information: family member profiles, memories, goals and reminders you choose to add.
  • Usage information: your conversations with the Rukik AI assistant, used to provide personalized answers.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information only to provide, maintain and improve the Service; to send reminders you set; and, with your authorization, to match you with professional service providers. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

3. Text Messaging (SMS)

Rukik sends one-time passcodes (OTP) by text message to verify your account and let you sign in. If you enter your mobile number in the Rukik app and request a verification code, you agree to receive these one-time passcode messages from Rukik.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories of data sharing above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. Your phone number and SMS opt-in consent are used solely to send you the verification codes you request, and are never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your explicit authorization, except: where required by law; to connect you with a professional you asked to be matched with, after your confirmation; and to process AI conversations through the AI service providers listed in the app, as necessary to provide the Service.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your data. You may export or delete your account data at any time from within the app.

6. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time in the app under My Family → Settings. After deletion, your personal data is removed from our servers.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: support@rukik.com
